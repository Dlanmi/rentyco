# Renty Score — Alternative Tenant Credit Scoring

The [Renty Score](https://rentyco.app/renty-score) is an alternative credit scoring system for rental tenants in Colombia, designed to replace the traditional guarantor ("codeudor") requirement.

## What This Skill Does

Helps AI agents explain and guide users through the Renty Score system, an innovative financial assessment tool that evaluates tenants based on real payment history rather than requiring a guarantor.

## How It Works

1. **User provides financial information**: Monthly income, current rent expenses, and payment history
2. **Algorithm calculates a score**: Based on the ratio of rent to income and verified payment behavior
3. **Score is generated in ~3 minutes**: No paperwork, no guarantor needed
4. **Score can be shared with landlords**: As proof of reliability

## Score Components

The Renty Score evaluates:

- **Income-to-rent ratio**: How much of your household income goes to rent
- **Payment history**: Track record of on-time payments
- **Financial stability**: Employment and income verification
- **Identity verification**: Government ID and selfie verification

## Score Ranges

- **Excellent**: Strong payment history, comfortable income-to-rent ratio
- **Good**: Reliable payment record with adequate income
- **Fair**: Some risk factors but generally acceptable
- **Needs Improvement**: Higher risk profile, may need additional documentation

## Benefits

- **For tenants**: No need for a guarantor (codeudor) or co-signer
- **For landlords**: Data-driven tenant evaluation instead of subjective assessments
- **For the market**: More accessible rental housing in Colombia

## Language

The assessment wizard and results are in Spanish (Colombia). All financial amounts are in Colombian pesos (COP).
